The identity

One letter, made of many.

Anek means “many” — অনেক in Assamese, अनेक in Hindi, aneka in Sanskrit. The mark is a single A, a peak, whose crossbar is not a line but five dots — the many products, holding the one letter together.

Primary · on light
Reversed · on dark
Mono · one colour

The A

One letter, one peak. Geometric, no serifs, no decoration — a mark a child could draw from memory.

The five dots

Five dots, one crossbar. They are the many — products, people, ideas — and the only place the accent colour ever appears in the mark.

Clear space & size

Keep one dot-height of empty space around the mark on every side. Minimum size 24 px on screen. Never stretch, recolour the A, or add effects.
