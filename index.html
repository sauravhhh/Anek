01 — The name

Anek means many.

One word, three languages, one idea. ANEK is the word for “many” — the many products, the many people they serve, the many small tools that add up to one company.

অনেক
Assamese · anek

“Many.” The word Saurabh grew up with — the natural name for a company that holds many products under one roof.

अनेक
Hindi & Sanskrit · aneka

“Many, various.” The same root, understood across India — a name that travels without translation.

Pronounced ah-NEK. Four letters, one syllable of work: short enough to remember, open enough to hold anything the company builds next.

02 — The logo

One letter, made of many.

The mark is a single A — the first letter of ANEK, and a peak: the ambition of the company. Its crossbar is not a line. It is five dots — the many products, holding the one letter together.

Primary · on light
Reversed · on dark
Mono · one colour

The A

One letter, one peak. Geometric, no serifs, no decoration — the same discipline as Meta and Alphabet: a mark a child could draw from memory.

The five dots

Five dots, one crossbar. They are the many — products, people, ideas — and they are the only place the accent colour ever appears in the mark.

Clear space & size

Keep one dot-height of empty space around the mark on every side. Minimum size 24 px on screen. Never stretch, recolour the A, or add effects.

One typeface, spoken plainly.

05 — Voice & principles

Small, honest, built to last.

Every ANEK product follows the same four principles. If a product breaks one, it is not finished yet.

01

Small, on purpose

Every product does one thing, and does it well. No feature bloat, no everything-app. Small is a choice, not a limitation.

02

Private by default

Work happens on the device wherever possible. No account walls, no tracking, no selling attention. The user’s data stays the user’s.

03

Built to last

No dark patterns, no ads in the core experience, no growth hacks that rot the product. A tool should still be worth opening in five years.

04

Many, not one

The parent stays quiet and the products speak. ANEK never crowds a product’s own name, colour, or voice.

Voice: plain words, short sentences, no hype. Say what it does, say what it costs, stop there. If a sentence needs an exclamation mark, rewrite the sentence.

06 — Brand architecture

One parent, four houses.

ANEK is an endorsed-brand parent, in the Alphabet model: the company owns the products, the products keep their own names, marks and colours. The parent appears only as a quiet endorsement — “by ANEK”.
