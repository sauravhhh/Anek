The identity

The word is the logo.

Anek means “many” — অনেক in Assamese, अनेक in Hindi, aneka in Sanskrit. The logo is the word itself — “ANEK” in capitals, set in Inter ExtraBold, finished with a Signal-orange full stop.

The word

The logo is the word itself — “ANEK” in capitals, in Inter ExtraBold, tracked tight. No symbol, no icon, nothing to decode.

The full stop

One orange full stop. It is the only colour in the logo — the dot that ends the word.

Clear space & size

Keep one letter-height of empty space around the wordmark on every side. Minimum size 24 px on screen. Never stretch, recolour it, or add effects.
